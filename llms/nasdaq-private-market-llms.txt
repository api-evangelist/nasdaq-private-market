# Nasdaq Private Market

> Nasdaq Private Market (NPM) operates infrastructure for the secondary market in pre-IPO private
> company equity — tender offers, company buybacks, auctions, block trades and a patented settlement
> engine for private share transfers — alongside a Data & Intelligence business built on NPM Price
> (a daily price estimate for 400+ liquid private companies) and Tape D (a private-company pricing
> dataset combining primary round data, secondary market transactions, mutual fund marks and 409A
> valuations across 4,000+ venture-backed companies). Securities are offered through NPM Securities,
> LLC (member FINRA/SIPC); SecondMarket Financial, LLC is an SEC-registered investment adviser and a
> wholly owned NPM subsidiary.

This file was generated by API Evangelist from Nasdaq Private Market's public surface. Nasdaq Private
Market does not publish an llms.txt of its own (https://www.nasdaqprivatemarket.com/llms.txt → 404).

## APIs

- [Tape D API](https://www.nasdaqprivatemarket.com/data-intelligence/): Private-company pricing and
  valuation data. Nasdaq, Inc. is the exclusive distributor; access is sold on NPM's Premium tier
  ("Talk to Sales") and delivered through Nasdaq Data Link's REST API plus SFTP/S3 bulk delivery.
  No OpenAPI, dataset code, or public API reference is published.

## Products

- [Investment Platform](https://www.nasdaqprivatemarket.com/investment-platform/): Buy and sell
  private company stock.
- [Data & Intelligence](https://www.nasdaqprivatemarket.com/data-intelligence/): NPM Price,
  financing history, waterfall analysis, portfolio analysis, sector analysis, source documents.
- [Liquidity](https://www.nasdaqprivatemarket.com/liquidity/): Tender offers, buybacks, auctions.
- [Settlement](https://www.nasdaqprivatemarket.com/settlement/): Patented private share transfer
  technology.
- [Capital Solutions](https://www.nasdaqprivatemarket.com/capital-solutions/): Primary raises and
  fund structures.
- [WealthConnect](https://www.nasdaqprivatemarket.com/wealth-connect/): Equity-specific tax and
  wealth advice.
